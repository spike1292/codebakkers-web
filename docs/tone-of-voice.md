# Tone of voice

How codebakkers.com talks. Use it for site copy, the CV page and post intros.

## In one line

Talk about the reader's teams and their result. Be modest about yourself and give credit to the team.

## Rules

1. **Start from the reader.** Open with their problem or the result they want: "Your teams keep shipping." Do not open with "I".
2. **"I help", "we did".** Use "I" for what I offer ("I help teams…"). Use "we" or "with my team" for what was done. Big results were never a solo act.
3. **No bragging.** No "I make 500 projects ship like one", "rockstar", "expert", "world-class". Let the facts carry the weight.
4. **Facts, not claims.** Use a real number or a concrete example in place of an adjective. "Two Angular versions with a one-day freeze" beats "seamless migrations".
5. **Plain words, short sentences.** Write for a busy manager and a senior engineer at the same time. Explain jargon or drop it.
6. **Concrete calls to action.** Say what happens next: "Let's plan the first step together." Not "Want this on your platform?".
7. **Warm, not salesy.** Friendly and direct, like a colleague who has done this before. No hype, no exclamation marks, no fake urgency.

## Examples

| Avoid                                          | Use                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I make 500 projects ship like one.             | Your teams keep shipping. Your platform moves forward.                                             |
| I moved 500 projects to standalone components. | With my team, we moved 500 projects to standalone components while 22 feature teams kept shipping. |
| Want this on your platform?                    | Is your frontend falling behind? Let's plan the first step together.                               |
| Proof, not promises.                           | What this looked like at Essent.                                                                   |

## Never

- The word "independent".
- Any job title other than "Frontend Platform Lead".
- Essent internals: cost figures, incidents, error counts, team rosters, ticket IDs.
