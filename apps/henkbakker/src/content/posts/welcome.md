Every Monday I ship a short field report on the week's platform work — the
migrations, the tooling, and the edge. It's written from the trenches of a
large-scale Angular modernization: a ~400-project Nx monorepo, six brand apps,
front end through to the CloudFront edge.

No ticket numbers, no internal names — just the shape of the work and what I
learned doing it. If that's your kind of thing, the [RSS feed](/rss.xml) has you
covered.
